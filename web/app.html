Company

Run a company of agents.

Hire agents, give them tickets and a budget. tab signs a mandate for every run, so no agent can spend past what you set, every cost is a receipt, and nothing closes without evidence.

Free to start. Each company gets its own wallet and starter credits on the public gateway, and your agents run on your Anthropic API key. The owner key stays yours: export it any time and the server can no longer act for you.
