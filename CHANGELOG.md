# Changelog

## 3.2.0 (2026-11)

tab inside the x402 market that already exists, buyers saving money they can see, more reasons to sell, and every charge publicly checkable. Protocol draft 0.9; the wire version stays `tab/0` and every new member is optional.

- **Install from GitHub releases.** Every release attaches the built package as `paidwithtab.tgz`, so `npm i -g https://github.com/paidwithtab/tab/releases/latest/download/paidwithtab.tgz` and `npx -y <that url> mcp` always get the newest tab. The README, the docs, the gateway home page, `tab init` and `tab setup` use it; `tab setup --npm` still writes the npm package name.
- **x402 sellers in the catalog.** `tab catalog serve --x402-list bazaar` reads the public x402 discovery list (about 33,000 payable routes from 3,200 sellers) into the catalog beside tab gateways; `tab_search` returns both, each result naming its `kind` and, for x402, its network and 30-day use. Only routes a tab client can pay are kept: `exact` EIP-3009 transfers of a known dollar token on a main network, up to $1 a call. A seller can add itself with `tab catalog add-x402 <origin>` (the catalog reads its `/.well-known/x402` and asks each resource its price). tab agents already pay x402 sites from their own address within the mandate; this makes them findable.
- **Base.** `examples/spec-base.json` and `examples/tab-gateway-base.service` run a gateway that settles in USDC on Base. The evm suite now checks, against Base itself, that the USDC signing domain tab computes is the one the contract verifies.
- **The cache of paid results.** The same GET inside ten minutes (or the response's own `Cache-Control: max-age`) is answered from its proof folder for nothing, and its proof still verifies. Other methods are reused only when the seller sets `max-age`, since a repeated POST may be meant to act twice. `no-store` is respected; `fresh: true` pays for a new answer; `TAB_CACHE_TTL` sets the window or turns it off.
- **Cost per task.** Every paid call is tagged with what the agent was working on: `TAB_TASK`, a `task` argument, or the git repository and branch. `tab cost --by task|day|realm|route` and the `tab_cost` tool sum the proof folders, with what the cache saved.
- **`tab_estimate`.** Prices a list of planned calls without paying and compares the total with the mandate: a GET by the challenge or x402 offer it gets back, any other method from the gateway's published terms, never sent.
- **The cheapest seller that works.** Gateways count each route's outcomes and time for seven days and publish them in discovery (`stats`); the catalog carries them into search with x402 sellers' recent paid use, ranks working sellers first, and takes `sort=cheapest|reliable`. A call that fails on the seller's side comes back with `alternatives` from `/v1/alternatives`.
- **Spending rules in mandates.** `paths` (route globs, optionally with a method) and `hours` (days and hours, UTC) are signed into the mandate and enforced by the gateway, on channel vouchers too, and by the client before it signs, x402 included. Sub-mandates may only narrow them. `tab mandate issue --paths "/search*" --hours "mon-fri 9-17"`; in tab OS, "Only these routes" and "Only these hours" on each agent. A mandate without rules keeps its id, and gateways before 3.2 refuse one with rules rather than ignore them. SPEC.md §2.3.
- **The public receipt log.** Every signed receipt is a leaf of an append-only Merkle tree (the RFC 9162 construction), with a tree head signed by the receipt key, inclusion proofs and consistency proofs at `/.well-known/tab/log`. `tab verify` checks that the receipt is in the log; `tab log check` keeps the last head it saw and proves the log only grew. Receipts from before 3.2 join the log in order on first start. SPEC.md §5.9.
- **Free calls.** `free_per_day` on a route or listing (`tab sell --free 3`) gives each buyer that many calls a day, counted per agent key and per address; a free call's receipt has `free: true`, amount 0 and the price the agent `authorized`.
- **Priced by size.** A metered route with `unit: "kb"` is charged for the response size the gateway counts itself; on the ledger rail per-call buyers now pay what a metered call used (the hold is the price, the rest goes back to the owner and the budget), and the receipt names the `units` and the price `authorized`.
- **Seller stats and sale notices.** `tab sell stats` signs a request for its listings' earnings, per route reliability and top buyers (shortened). A listing with `notify` (`tab sell --notify url` or `--notify-ntfy`) is told of its first sale and each day's earnings.
- **Card top-ups.** `tab serve --stripe` (with `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) opens a Stripe Checkout Session for $1 to $100 and credits the ledger wallet once when Stripe's signed webhook says it was paid; no card data reaches the gateway. `tab topup 5`.
- **Referrals.** With `--referrals`, a starter-credit claim may name a referrer, who earns 10% of what that buyer spends from card top-ups, on up to $5 of it.
- **Usage stats.** `GET /.well-known/tab/stats` (bearer `TAB_STATS_TOKEN`, off without it) and `tab stats` count outside users with the operator's own addresses and owners left out (`--house-ip`, `--house-owner`, and the gateway's own loopback).
- **`tab setup <claude|cursor|windsurf|vscode|codex>`** writes the tab MCP server into that tool's config, keeping what is there. docs/integrations.md covers the OpenAI Agents SDK, the Vercel AI SDK and LangChain. tab OS shows the commands under Integrations.
- `tab verify` with no folder checks the latest purchase. New MCP tools `tab_estimate` and `tab_cost` come after the existing ones. New suite `test:v32` (127 checks); the evm suite reads Base.

## 3.1.0 (2026-11)

Useful in the first minute, for buyers, sellers and owners. Protocol draft 0.8; the wire version stays `tab/0` and every new member is optional.

- **One line to start.** `tab mcp` sets itself up on first launch when nothing is there: keys, a $1.00 a day mandate and the public gateway's starter credits, reported on stderr so stdout stays the protocol. `claude mcp add --scope user tab -- npx -y paidwithtab mcp` is the whole setup. `TAB_GATEWAY` points it (and `tab init`) at another gateway.
- **Proof of purchase.** `tab fetch` and the MCP server keep each paid response in `~/.tab/receipts/<time>-<id>/` with its signed receipt, the request and the mandate (`TAB_PROOFS` moves it or turns it off; `tab_fetch` returns the folder). `tab verify` checks the gateway's signature and published key, the body hash, the request, the agent's own signature on the payment and the owner's on the mandate, offline or online; `tab receipts` lists them. SPEC.md §3.4.
- **Hosted listings.** `tab sell <https url> --price 0.01 [--route "GET /path [price]"]...` signs a listing and posts it to a gateway that takes them (`tab serve --listings`); it is served under `/s/<slug>/`, listed in discovery and the catalog, and pays the seller's own wallet, or on an evm gateway the seller's 0x address straight from the agent's transfer. Services may now name their own `recipient` and `evm_recipient`. `tab sell list`, `tab sell remove`. SPEC.md §5.7.
- **MCP servers, sold per tool call.** `tab sell <MCP url> --mcp --price 0.005 [--tool-price name=0.02]` makes one paid route per tool; the gateway makes the JSON-RPC call (JSON or SSE, with the session id), and a tool that reports an error is not charged. `tab mcp connect <listing url>` gives Claude Code those tools as its own, paid from the mandate. `tab mcp http -- <command>` puts a stdio MCP server on HTTP so it can be sold.
- **Spend alerts.** `tab alerts set (--url https://... | --ntfy) [--at 50,90,100] [--daily]` signs a subscription at each gateway (`tab serve --alerts`): a post when a mandate crosses a share of its budget in its window, once per threshold, and a daily statement. `--ntfy` makes a private ntfy.sh topic, so alerts reach a phone with no account. SPEC.md §5.8.
- **Egress is guarded.** Hosted upstreams and webhooks are called only at public https addresses, checked when connecting, with no redirects; listings are rate limited per listing and capped per seller, per address and in total, and signed listings and subscriptions are bound to one gateway and a 10 minute window. x402 and channel vouchers are refused on hosted routes.
- New suite `test:v31` (69 checks), and the evm suite pays a hosted listing on chain.

## 3.0.1 (2026-11)

Security release from a full audit of the gateway, tab OS and the deployment. Wire compatible with 3.0.0.

- **Client: a challenge is paid only to the realm it came from.** A 402 claiming another realm than the host fetched is refused before anything is signed (loopback and `.local` hosts excepted). On the evm rail every signed transfer is counted against the whole mandate chain before it leaves. Before, a hostile site could answer with an allowed gateway's realm and collect signed transfers.
- **Revocations hold under load.** Pending revocations are never evicted (a flood used to push out a real one and bring a revoked mandate back), are kept compactly for at most 30 days, and a full table refuses new entries with 503. A revocation counts as known only for a mandate this gateway was paid under; peer rows are checked the same way.
- **The revocation list no longer publishes mandate terms.** Peers configured with `--peer` still receive the full document.
- **Bounded upstream bodies.** Upstream answers are streamed with a 32 MB cap (`maxUpstreamBytes`); past it the call is a 502 and nothing is charged.
- **Catalog announces are limited**: per IP rate, at most 2,000 origins, failed newcomers are not stored, refresh runs 8 at a time, and a newcomer's discovery document is capped at 4 MB.
- **Smaller fixes.** `payment-signature` and `x-payment` are not forwarded upstream. RPC errors name the host only, and evm wallet and nonce lookups are rate limited. At most 200 approvals may wait per owner. Gateway home pages send `nosniff`, `frame-ancestors 'none'` and a referrer policy. `tab serve` and `tab catalog serve` take `--listen 127.0.0.1`.
- **tab OS.** Orders count only at the company's own gateways, so a page an agent fetches can no longer claim a refund and erase spend from the books. A ticket done or cancelled stays that way when a late run reports, and only the assignee (or the colleague a ticket was handed to) moves a ticket; a mentioned colleague adds evidence. A payment approved while the asking run is still finishing is no longer lost. The runs per ticket per hour cap applies to every ticket, not only those with a budget. Starter credits go to at most two companies per account and gateway, and to a capped number a day.

## 3.0.0 (2026-11)

Control after the mandate is signed. Protocol draft 0.7; the wire version stays `tab/0` and every new member is optional, so 2.x mandates, credentials, receipts and clients keep working unchanged.

- **Instant revoke.** Owners (or the delegating agent, or the agent itself) sign a revocation; gateways refuse the mandate and everything delegated from it. `POST`/`GET /.well-known/tab/revocations`, gateways share them with `--peer` (push, and polling every 5 s). `tab mandate revoke`, `tab mandate status`.
- **Sub-mandates.** A mandate may carry its whole `parent` and be signed by the parent's agent. Every limit must sit inside the parent's, spend counts against every level, chains stop at 3 delegations. `tab mandate delegate`, MCP `tab_delegate`.
- **Approval above a threshold.** `approve_above` on a mandate; larger payments get an `approval-required` problem with the request to sign, and pay once with the owner's approval attached or posted to the gateway. `tab mandate issue --approve-above`, `tab mandate approve`, `tab fetch --wait-approval`.
- **Pay on delivery.** Routes with `escrow` hold an opted-in buyer's payment until the upstream delivers (202 now, `POST` to `Tab-Deliver` later), then release it with a delivery receipt; refunds on failure or deadline. On the evm rail through a gateway-held escrow address (`--escrow-key`). `tab fetch --escrow`, `tab escrow status`, MCP `tab_escrow`.
- **Payment channels and metered routes.** One deposit, cumulative single-use vouchers per call, one settlement at close; routes with `meter` charge per reported unit, below a cent if need be. `tab channel open|list|close`, `tab fetch --channel`, MCP `tab_channel`. `contracts/TabChannel.sol` is a trustless channel contract with a dispute window, tested on a local EVM and deployable to testnets with `tab evm deploy-channel`; gateways do not settle through it yet.
- **tab OS.** Revoke on runs, agents and delegated budgets (pause and retire revoke runs in flight); managers' sub-tickets run on delegated budgets shown on their cards; payment approvals in the Inbox with phone sized Approve and Deny, and the agent resumes after approval; orders paid on delivery in Receipts; payment channels in the Wallet; hire requests carry the new agent's model and per call cap.
- **Fixes.** Every challenge carries a random `nonce`: two buyers asking for the same route in the same millisecond used to get one challenge id that only the first could redeem. A gateway that charges in its own token names it in every amount (the 402 detail used to say USD), in budget and cap refusals on both sides, and in wallet lookups.
- **Security review fixes.** Pay on delivery and channels need `--origin`, and escrow tokens are bound to it: a buyer's Host header can no longer steer the seller's delivery. Payouts out of held money are idempotent per key (the same signed transaction is rebroadcast, never a second one) and an order never reopens to a second outcome. Locks into escrow must confirm, with late arrivals sent back. Delivered results are served as sandboxed attachments, and echoed delivery tokens are redacted. Channel lists respect `public_receipts`, and stuck payouts back off and stop. TabChannel ids are single use. Approvals dated in the future are refused, and spent approvals are remembered until they expire. Revocations are taken only when a mandate authorizes them, rate limited and batched, with peers polling by sequence. `tab mandate status` checks authority itself. x402 payments respect approval thresholds and the whole chain. `tab_delegate` is opt-in. On evm, revoking also zeroes allowances (CLI and tab OS), and the docs say plainly that the allowance is the on-chain cap.
- `tab version`. New suites `test:v3` and `test:contract`.
